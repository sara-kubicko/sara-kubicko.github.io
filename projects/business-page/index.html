Little Market

A groovy gathering place with artisanal coffee and amazing food, centered in Old Ellicott City.

Our coffee

30 years ago a bond was formed between the owners of Northbound Coffee Roasters and the owner of the Little Market Cafe. Neither were in the coffee business back then. They were in the traveling music festival business, selling their jewelry & clothing at concerts around the country.

Little did either know they would be working together again three decades later. With the help of the internet the two were reunited only to find out they had both settled in small towns near historic railroad stations but at opposite sides of America!

Northbound Coffee is roasted in small (tiny, really) batches in their vintage roaster at the base of Mount Shasta in beautiful Northern California.

They only roast to order, as they believe that the freshest coffee is the best coffee.

Our food

We are proud to serve a variety of fresh, healthy, and delicious food options. Our menu features a range of sandwiches, salads, and snacks made with locally sourced ingredients. We also offer vegetarian and vegan options to cater to all dietary preferences.

Whether you're looking for a quick bite or a leisurely meal, our cafe provides a welcoming atmosphere for all.

Our location

Located in the heart of Old Ellicott City, our cafe is easily accessible and offers a cozy ambiance for our guests. We are just a short walk from the historic district, making it a perfect stop for tourists and locals alike.

Come visit us and enjoy the charm of our town while savoring our delightful offerings.
